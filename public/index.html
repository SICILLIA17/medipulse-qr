System Engineering & Strategic Roadmap

How to Make MediPulse QR Better

Comprehensive medical gateway architectures designed for hospital-grade resilience, zero-connectivity disaster response, and EHR interoperability.

1

Zero-Connectivity Cryptographic QR (Offline PWA)

Compress critical emergency data directly inside the QR code using CBOR and Base45 with ECDSA digital signatures, enabling paramedics to triage with zero cellular signal.

2

"Break-Glass" Emergency Access & Family Notifications

Log GPS coordinates and dispatch an automated SMS to the patient proxy whenever emergency triage access occurs.

3

Two-Tier Granular Privacy Architecture

Public tier displays blood type and anaphylaxis; authenticated clinical tier unlocks psychiatric notes and prescription titrations.

4

SMART-on-FHIR & HL7 Hospital Systems Bridge

Bi-directional integration with Epic Systems and Cerner EHR platforms via standard FHIR v4 APIs.

5

Bedside AI Drug-Drug & Allergy Conflict Engine

Automated safety checker alerts physicians if prescribing a medication that conflicts with an existing allergy or anticoagulant.

6

Dynamic Anti-Tamper Rotating QR & NFC Smart Band

TOTP rotating QR codes (refreshed every 60s) paired with an embedded NFC chip for tap-to-open verification.
